# ReAI RecMan

Native Apple Silicon macOS 15+ app for reviewing RecMan employee, invoice, credit note, and payroll records and importing them into ReAI.

## Connect

1. Build with `tools/apple/build.py recman` using Swift 6.4 and open `dist/recman/ReAI RecMan.app`.
2. Open **Settings** and set **ReAI URL** to a loopback root such as `http://localhost:8080` or to `https://app.reai.no`. The ReAI server must advertise the same root in its device-approval URL.
3. Select **Connect to ReAI** and approve the NOK company. Your ReAI access determines the records and actions available.
4. Open **Settings** and select **Set RecMan Key…** to save the key. **Test RecMan** checks read access and identifies the RecMan organization when the key exposes it. Grant SELECT on candidates, companies, products, invoices, credit notes, payroll, work, and projects for the corresponding workflows.

## Import

- **Employees & Payroll.** Load employees, check the ones to import, then select **Review Import**. The preview includes only their unexported paychecks. The review shows how many paychecks are ready and which confirmations are missing. It reuses a saved municipality when it matches the verified workplaces. The app fills clear wage mappings based on the RecMan article name and type. It uses supplement codes advertised by the connected ReAI server. Review the suggested mappings and choose wage types for ambiguous articles, including overtime, holiday work, and standby. Review NOK and the workplace municipality before importing. If the linked workplaces resolve to one municipality, the preview prefills an editable code wherever the ReAI employment municipality is missing. The app looks up the linked companies’ publicly registered workplace street and postcode using Kartverket. If no address can be resolved, enter a verified municipality manually. When workplaces resolve to multiple municipalities, verify how the employment should be recorded in ReAI. The app derives the payroll month from the wage lines and uses the company's ReAI payout setting to propose a payment date. It uses the last calendar day of the payroll month when no setting exists and retains the payment date of a draft it created earlier.
- **Import Employees & Payroll** adds or matches employees and employment relations, then adds ready wage lines to native ReAI drafts. The employee import sends names, emails, and employment dates and percentages. It fills blank bank accounts from RecMan when a Norwegian account passes validation and shows the last four digits in the preview. If payroll still needs review, **Save Employee Updates** saves independently confirmed municipalities and other ready employee changes. **Import Ready Records** imports ready drafts and shows how many paychecks remain held. ReAI applies defaults for other unset employment fields. Paychecks with incomplete mappings or prerequisites stay in review. If this app already created a draft for the month, it can append wage lines for the employees already on that draft.
- **Invoices.** Choose an inclusive start date and review the invoices and credit notes. The app suggests customer matches or creates verified Norwegian business customers with the import. The preview shows original line descriptions, amounts, revenue accounts and VAT codes. The app uses accounting codes from the issued lines when supplied, with the RecMan product as the fallback for missing codes. Confirm NOK and book the selected documents. ReAI creates a source order and books the invoice or credit note with the original PDF and reminders disabled. For paid invoices, the preview identifies a single incoming NOK bank transaction by the original KID and full amount. The approved import registers the receipt and reconciles its bank posting, or reuses the posting of an existing matching payment on the invoice. Missing, ambiguous, already matched, or otherwise unverified payments stay in review. Documents with incompatible codes or amounts stay in review. Credit notes remain unapplied.

Check records and confirm the operation and destination before each import. The app does not send invoices or EHF, submit A-melding, or execute payments.

## Local state and recovery

RecMan and ReAI credentials use separate Keychain items under `no.reai.recman`. ReAI approvals and import journals are scoped to the selected server. Changing the server clears the preview and restores its approval, when available. Disconnect removes the credential for that server.

RecMan access is GET-only and user-triggered. Under `~/Library/Application Support/ReAIRecMan/`, the app keeps a 24-hour cache of companies, products, and projects and counts up to 200 requests per configured key per Oslo calendar day. Journals retain per-record receipts and advance incremental checkpoints only through a range with no unimported eligible records. Employee input, document PDFs, and payroll previews stay in memory.

A pre-write receipt records the operation and digest before each POST. A write with an uncertain outcome blocks additional writes to that ReAI company. Check the destination record and reconcile the journal before resuming. Do not delete the journal or retry against a fresh profile to work around an uncertain write. When a previously imported employee has been deleted in ReAI, the preview offers **Recreate missing employee**. Confirm the deletion before importing. The app retains the old receipt and records the new import separately.

Local builds are for development. Publish a distributable build through the shared signing and notarization workflow after review and merge.
