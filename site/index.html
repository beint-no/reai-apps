GET STARTED

Download. Connect. Work.

No developer tools or API-key copying.

  1. 01

    Install the app

    Open the downloaded disk image and drag the app to Applications.

  2. 02

    Connect to ReAI

    Sign in in your browser, compare the code, choose a company and approve.

  3. 03

    Start working

    The app uses your existing permissions in the company you selected.

Your password stays in the browser. Access keys stay in macOS Keychain.
Revoke an app’s access at any time in your ReAI profile.
