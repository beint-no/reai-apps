GET STARTED

Download. Connect. Work.

Connect the app to ReAI in your browser and approve one company.

  1. 01

    Install

    Mac: open the disk image and drag the app to Applications.
    Windows: extract the whole ZIP and open the app’s EXE.

  2. 02

    Connect to ReAI

    Click Connect to ReAI, sign in in your browser, compare the code and approve one company.

  3. 03

    Start working

    The app uses your existing permissions in the company you selected.

Windows downloads

Choose x64 for Intel/AMD PCs or ARM64 for ARM-based PCs. Extract the whole ZIP before opening the app; no build tools or administrator access are needed.

These apps are unsigned. If SmartScreen offers More info → Run anyway, you can choose it after confirming the download is from our GitHub repository and you trust it. Shipping and Document Scanner update themselves via Check for updates.

Your password stays in the browser. Access keys stay in macOS Keychain or Windows Credential Locker.
Revoke an app’s access at any time in your ReAI profile.
