## Bring Norwegian field & shift hours into ReAI

Construction, trades, retail and hospitality teams using **Tidsbanken**, **SmartDok**, **Busy** or **Planday** can export their approved monthly hours as CSV and import them directly into ReAI Timesheets for payroll and project billing.

## Step-by-step setup

1. Export approved hours as CSV from Tidsbanken, SmartDok or Busy (including Date/Dato and Hours/Timer columns).
2. Click **Open integration**, connect your ReAI company, pick target employee and project, and drop the CSV file.
3. Review every line and post directly into ReAI Timesheets.
