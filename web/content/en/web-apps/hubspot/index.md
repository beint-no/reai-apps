## Keep sales and invoicing aligned without paid CRM connectors

Sales teams live in HubSpot while finance invoices in ReAI. Re-typing customer names, addresses and organization numbers leads to broken EHF/Peppol deliveries and duplicate customer cards. With this integration you scan your HubSpot companies on demand, fill in any missing Norwegian organization number inline, and create verified customer records directly in ReAI—without paying for Zapier or third-party CRM bridges.
