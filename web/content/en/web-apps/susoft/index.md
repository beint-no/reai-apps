## How Susoft integrates with ReAI

Many Norwegian businesses pay monthly third-party middleware fees just to move data from **Susoft** into their accounting system. ReAI provides a free, open-source browser integration at `apps.reai.no/app/susoft/` using ReAI's documented Public API.

| Dimension | Mapping in ReAI |
| --- | --- |
| Target industry | Omnichannel retail & gift cards (Butikk) |
| Norwegian ledger / API | `1900, 1580, 3000, 2900` |
| Safety & auditability | Preview before booking, duplicate protection & accountant review workflow |

## Getting started

1. Click **Open integration** above and connect your ReAI company (your password never leaves `app.reai.no`).
2. Try the built-in **Load sample** button to inspect the exact postings or sync preview immediately.
3. Upload your Susoft export or enter your credentials/settlement totals to post directly into ReAI.
