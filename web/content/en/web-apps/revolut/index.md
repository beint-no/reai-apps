## Bookkeeping for Revolut Business & Wise in Norway

Norwegian SaaS startups, ecommerce brands and consultants often pay foreign SaaS tools and suppliers using **Revolut Business** or **Wise Business**. This integration parses your CSV statement, isolates FX/transfer fees onto account `7770`, and builds a balanced monthly review voucher against account `1925`.

## Step-by-step with ReAI

1. Download a monthly CSV statement from **Revolut Business** or **Wise Business**.
2. Click **Open integration**, connect your ReAI company, confirm your ledger account (`1925`) and drop the CSV.
3. Review every expense and fee line, then create the review voucher in ReAI.
