## From paper and screen to ReAI

Document Scanner sends files to the ReAI company you approve when you connect. Choose where each file goes: Documents, Supplier invoices, Income reception or Receipt reception. ReAI analyzes reception documents for review; uploading never registers or pays anything.

## Ways to add documents

- **Capture a receipt.** Press Cmd+Shift+B on Mac or Ctrl+Shift+B on Windows from any app, select the receipt on screen, and it goes to Receipt reception. A notification confirms the upload. You can change the Mac shortcut in Settings.
- **Scan.** On Mac, use Continuity Camera to take a photo or scan documents with your iPhone or iPad, or use a USB or network scanner. On Windows, use any scanner that Windows recognizes. Pages from one scan session are combined into one PDF.
- **Drop files.** Drop PDFs and images into the window or on the menu bar icon (Mac). On Windows, the tray icon opens a small drop panel that stays on top.
- **Watch folders.** Link a folder, for example your scanner or downloads folder, to a company and destination. New files upload while the app is running. Files already in the folder are skipped.

## Safe uploads

Originals stay untouched; the app keeps a local copy and a history of every upload. A file with the same contents is uploaded only once per company and destination. If an upload is interrupted, the app marks it **Check ReAI** and only retries after you confirm, so the same document is not created twice. Files ReAI rejects can be retried directly.

ReAI accepts PDF, PNG, JPEG, AVIF, BMP and GIF, up to 20 MB per file. The Mac app converts HEIC, TIFF and WebP images to JPEG first.

## Moving from Finder Vault

Document Scanner replaces ReAI Finder Vault for Mac. Quit Finder Vault, install Document Scanner and open it. Your upload history, watched folders, company inbox folders and local copies move over automatically. macOS may ask whether Document Scanner may use the Finder Vault key in Keychain; allow it to keep your connection, or connect to ReAI again. Then delete Finder Vault from Applications.

## Access and privacy

The app connects only to ReAI. Your password stays in the browser, and the access key is stored in macOS Keychain or Windows Credential Locker. It only works for the company you approved and uses your existing permissions. Revoke it at any time in your [ReAI profile](https://app.reai.no/user/profile#user-access-tokens).
